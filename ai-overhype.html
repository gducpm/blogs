AI Is Becoming WAY TOO OVERHYPED

Why has AI become so overhyped? And why SHOULDN'T you rely on it? Find out!

By: GDucpm | First posted: 2026-11-21 | Last revised: 2026-11-21 | Last updated: 2026-11-21

Why? Because a lot of people want to do things they have never done before

I know some people who have never done programming before. I use Artificial Intelligence sometimes because there are simply things that I want made real, fast, without effort learning any programming. I once created games using PHP with Artificial Intelligence (they were never released – I canceled them), because I was busy as hell and I could not afford to learn PHP again and program the game. Also, I have never drawn anything good, I'm not an artist. (Generating images with Artificial Intelligence is plagiarism and insults artists; this case is much different from programming, see why below.)

Most Artificial Intelligence providers "use your chats to train their models"

Which sounds bad already. They have a toggle to "disable using your chats to train their models", but looking at the wording I guess they only stop training models with your chats; they may use them in so many other ways. To be sure, you'd have to read through the AI provider's ToS which is 99% pure legalese that even I cannot understand.

Please be cautious when using AI. AI providers first, Big Data second, are the ones who will get your chats and analyze your interests. This is invasive and bad to your digital data sovereignty.

Using Artificial Intelligence can be plagiarism

...If you use it in stuff that are supposed to be right-brained. Artists complain that AI has made them lose their jobs. I have a lot of friends who are artists, and they all absolutely hate AI. Please do not fall into the trap of using Artificial Intelligence to generate Artificial Intelligence IMAGES, MUSIC, OR LITERATURE. At least, try your best to use your own wording for everything, even if you're not a native English speaker.

Artificial Intelligence-generated images, music and literature can be absolutely HIDEOUS

Since it's trying to do right-brained stuff with its left brain, it's going to be hardly enjoyable. Some people may love images created using Artificial Intelligence, but do you see? Mistakes that artists, musicians and poets never make. Things that are way too perfect. Things that aren't human can look soulless, hideous.

Code, Math and Science (a.k.a. Maths) are different from Arts

Code, math, and science are just maths in general. They have something in common: they're not creative. They're left-brained stuff, which means programming with AI isn't plagiarism. It's not the same case like art and music.

Schools are introducing AI Skills Education

My school is also organizing a lot of events, school projects and blah blah blah related to AI Programming in general (such as vibe-coding, which is just one kind). This discourages actual programming; although this might be about AI prompting skills, I think actual programming, even if it's in Scratch, would be much better since it encourages logical thinking and human intelligence, which is what a school should do to their students. So yes, AI is being overhyped at my school.

Vibe-coding is kinda dangerous

Vibe coding is a branch of AI Programming, meaning "let AI program without human understanding of the code". This is harmful; you should check your code and understand it so you have full sovereignty over what it does. Ask AI about what this does, what that does, and try to understand them, because you don't want to be left in the dark, left stupid and helpless. Instead, please do Supervised & Reviewed AI Programming.

Richard M. Stallman: "I define "intelligence" as being capable of knowing or understanding..."

"...at least within some domain. ChatGPT cannot know or understand anything, so it is not intelligence. It does not know what its output means. It has no idea that words can mean anything."

He goes on to call it a "bullsh*t generator", and I completely agree. AI generation is completely statistical, and when it says something, it cannot erase its own words. It does not have a second checkup pass or anything. This is why some AI implementations have a thinking process to just be mildly thoughtful.
